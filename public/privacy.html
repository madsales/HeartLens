What HeartLens processes

HeartLens analyzes only the text you paste into it: a profile, a conversation, and an optional note about yourself. That text is sent to our server and forwarded to a third-party AI provider (Anthropic) to produce the analysis.

What we store

The server does not store the text you submit or the results it returns. Your browser keeps a local draft of what you typed so a page refresh doesn't lose it; clearing site data removes it.

Other people's information

The text you paste is usually about someone else. Only share what you have a reasonable basis to share, and remove names, contact details, or anything else that isn't needed for the analysis.

Results

Results are probability-based estimates, not facts. They can be wrong, and they are not a substitute for your own judgment.
